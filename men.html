Frequently Asked Questions

The stuff women ask before they sign up. Straight answers, no fluff.

I've tried everything. Will this actually work? +

That's why we built it. The reason you've failed before isn't you — it's that every program you've tried was built for someone whose life looks nothing like yours. We start with the reality of your week, not a fantasy version of it.

I've got zero time. Genuinely. +

Then you're exactly who this is built for. Short, focused sessions. Meals that don't require a Sunday meal-prep marathon. The whole system is designed around women with careers, families, and limited hours.

Do I have to go keto / carnivore / no carbs? +

No. No extreme diets. You'll eat real food, still enjoy the occasional glass of wine, and get leaner every week. If a coach tells you a food is 'bad,' they're selling you religion, not results.

What if I'm 10kg over? What if I'm 40kg over? +

We've coached both ends of that spectrum and everyone in between. The system doesn't change based on how much you have to lose — the timeline does.

What about Ozempic? +

That's a chat for your GP. What I'll tell you is this: quick fixes lead to quick failures. The weight comes back the second you stop. You're not broken because you haven't found the right pill — you're stuck because you haven't built a system.

Is this for men too? +

No — this page is the women's program. Men should check out the men's side. The physiology's different and we don't do one-size-fits-all.
